Methodology & caveats: Amazon bestseller pages cover the "utility, solves one annoyance" archetype; Amazon's regular keyword search (a fixed watchlist of known-viral terms) covers "novelty/gift/room-decor" instead, since Amazon.in has no bestseller page for those subcategories. Neither has an official trending-products API, so either can break silently if Amazon changes its layout. Flipkart/Meesho/Myntra/Snapdeal are currently disabled (config.ENABLED_SOURCES) after confirming they're blocked even from cloud IPs — their tables above simply won't appear while disabled. "Picks" scoring is a heuristic (bestseller rank + Google Trends token overlap), gated by the 13-KPI rubric judged per-candidate by Gemini, capped at 20 requests/day (free tier) — which is why the candidate pool and category/keyword lists are sized to fit that ceiling rather than maximize scrape volume. The Alibaba/AliExpress columns only confirm a matching listing exists and shares words with the query — not price, MOQ, or seller reliability, so vet a supplier manually before committing.
